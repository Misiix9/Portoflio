Support

Presso · by Selora

Presso compresses videos and photos to the size you actually need: under 25 MB for email, 10 MB for Discord, WhatsApp-ready, or any size you pick. Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and there is no subscription.

Need help? Email support@selora.dev.

If you're reporting a problem, please include your macOS version and roughly what the file was (length, resolution, camera or app it came from). That's usually enough to reproduce it.

How do I compress something?

Drag a file onto the Presso window or the menu bar icon, pick where it's going, and press Compress. You can also press ⌘O to choose files, or right-click a file in Finder and choose Compress with Presso. The smaller copy is saved next to the original, or wherever you choose in Settings.

Does it change my original file?

Never. Presso always writes a new copy and leaves the original untouched. If you switch on Move originals to the Trash in Settings, the original goes to the Trash after a successful compression, where you can still recover it.

What does Presso Pro unlock?

Unlimited compressions, GIF and audio export, HEIC photos and any-size photo targets, saved presets, a watch folder that compresses automatically, turbo batches, and the full resolution and frame-rate controls in Custom. It's a single payment, never a subscription, and it's shared with your family through Family Sharing. The first three compressions are free so you can check the results before paying.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

Open Settings ▸ Presso Pro and choose Restore purchase, making sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. This is also how you unlock Pro on a second Mac.

What can I compress?

Videos in MP4, MOV, M4V and most other formats macOS can read, and photos in JPEG, PNG and HEIC. Compressed videos come out as MP4 (H.264, or HEVC on the max-quality preset), photos as JPEG or, with Pro, HEIC.

Can I keep only part of a video?

Yes. Press the scissors on a queued file to set a start and an end, and only that part is compressed. This is free, and it's also the way to fit a long clip into a GIF, which tops out at 30 seconds.

What happens to my photos' location data?

It's removed. Presso writes fresh image data and never copies the original's metadata, so GPS coordinates, camera details and timestamps stay behind. The copy you send carries the picture and nothing else. Your original file keeps everything.

Why is the result bigger or smaller than the estimate?

The estimate uses the same maths as the encoder, so it's usually within a few percent, but how much a video compresses depends on what's in it: fast motion and grain need more data than a static shot. Presso checks the finished file and re-encodes once if it landed over the limit, so a preset's promise holds.

Can it compress a folder automatically?

With Pro, yes. In Settings ▸ Automation choose a watch folder: anything new you drop in there gets compressed on its own with your current preset. Presso waits until the file has finished copying before touching it, and never re-compresses its own output.

Is my data private?

Yes. Presso has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network requests at all. Your files never leave your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

Refunds
