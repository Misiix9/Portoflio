Privacy Policy

Presso · by Selora · last updated 25 July 2026

The short version: Presso does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no network requests of any kind.

What Presso stores, and where

Your videos and photos stay on your Mac. Compression happens entirely on-device using Apple's built-in video and image frameworks, and the compressed copy is written to the folder you chose, next to the original by default.

Presso keeps a few small preferences on your Mac: your selected preset, output folder, saved presets, and a local history of finished compressions (file name and before/after sizes) so it can show how much space you've saved. That list lives only on your Mac and can be cleared at any time in Settings ▸ History.

We (Selora) cannot see, access or retrieve any of this. It never leaves your machine.

Presso has no servers and no user accounts.

Network access

Presso makes no outbound network requests. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The app works exactly the same with your Mac offline. The only network activity connected to Presso is the App Store itself when you download the app or make a purchase, which is handled by Apple, not by us.

Photo metadata

When Presso compresses a photo it writes fresh image data and does not copy the original's metadata. GPS coordinates, camera information and timestamps are therefore not present in the copy you send. Your original file is never modified and keeps all of its metadata.

Purchases

Presso Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Presso never sees your name, card or Apple Account details. Purchase status is verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payments.

Files you give Presso access to

Presso runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can only read the files you drop in or choose, and can only write to the output folder you pick. If you enable the watch folder, macOS grants Presso access to that one folder so it can compress new files placed there. You can revoke that at any time by choosing a different folder or turning the feature off.

Data collection summary

Presso's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected." The app's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Children

Presso is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
