Privacy Policy

iPhone and iPad · Android

Ora · by Selora · last updated 30 October 2026

Your work records stay on your device or in your private iCloud account. Ora has no app account and no in-app advertising. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information to verify and restore Ora Pro; RevenueCat also provides purchase analytics.

Your work records

Jobs, pay rates, shifts, breaks, tips, notes, goals and settings are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, supported work records are also stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and sync between your devices signed in to that Apple Account. Saved device-local reviews and exported files may require their own backup; the app identifies those separately.

Ora does not automatically send your work ledger to Selora or RevenueCat. If you choose to export or share a file, it is sent to the destination you select. If you send records to support, Selora can see the information you choose to include.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Ora Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment. Ora does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Ora uses Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat to check purchase status, unlock features, restore access and help prevent purchase fraud. These access checks can occur when you open Ora, including before you make a purchase. RevenueCat processes purchase history, receipts and transaction information, an app user identifier generated by its SDK, and technical request context such as app/device/operating-system information and locale. The app does not set that identifier to your name or email, and does not send your shifts, wages, notes or location to RevenueCat.

Purchase information is used for app functionality and purchase analytics, including RevenueCat's customer-history and reporting tools. Ora does not use in-app advertisements or cross-company advertising tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, its Apple privacy guidance and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Exchange rates

When you open Earnings, Ora may refresh public exchange rates from the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu) for currency-conversion features. This request does not contain your work ledger. Network providers receive the technical information needed to deliver their responses.

Location, reminders and diagnostics

If you enable workplace reminders, Ora uses location on your device to offer clock-in or clock-out reminders. The saved workplace location and reminder radius are part of a job record: they are included in JSON backups and can sync to your private iCloud when sync is enabled. Ora does not send your workplace coordinates or live location to Selora or RevenueCat. Arrival and departure checks offer reminders; they do not automatically record attendance. Notifications use the preferences you choose.

Ora's on-device diagnostic reports are kept locally; the app does not upload those reports to Selora. Apple may separately process diagnostics under your device and App Store settings.

Support and privacy choices

You can manage location and notification permissions in iOS Settings, choose whether to use iCloud, export your records, and use Ora's data-erasure controls. Erasing local app data does not cancel an App Store purchase or erase records held by Apple. Contact support about purchase-service records or information you have voluntarily sent to us.

If you email support, we receive your email and the information you include. You may provide Ora's copied Account ID so we can investigate purchase access. Include only the information needed for your request; a full work ledger is not required to troubleshoot Pro ownership.

App Store privacy details

Ora uses RevenueCat for purchase processing, so its privacy disclosure includes Purchase History for App Functionality and Analytics. Work records and location processed only on your device are separate from purchase information processed by the purchase service. The App Store privacy details describe the submitted app and its third-party services.

Children

Ora is not directed at children. It does not require app sign-up or ask for a child's name or age. Purchase processing is described above.

Android

Last updated 10 November 2026

Ora keeps your work records on your Android device. You can choose to make copies or share reports. Ora does not require an Ora account or carry in-app advertising.

Work records and files

Ora keeps your jobs, shifts, breaks, rates, estimated earnings, notes, goals and settings on your device. When you create a backup or report, you choose where to save or share it. You can optionally let Ora check for a weekly backup when you open it and save a full ledger copy to a folder you choose. Those copies include earnings, notes and saved workplace coordinates even when Hide earnings is on. Turning off weekly backups leaves existing copies in the selected folder.

To remove your ledger, open Ora Settings and choose Erase all data. Ora clears work records, private pay reviews and its document caches on this device. Clearing Ora's storage or uninstalling Ora also removes the device copy. Other apps, folder providers and people you share with may retain copies. Manage saved backups and reports in the locations you chose for them.

Optional Google Drive sync

If Drive sync is available in your version and you enable it, Ora sends supported work records and their changes to its hidden application folder in your Google Drive. Other Android devices running Ora can read those changes after you connect the same Google Account. Ora requests access to its own application folder. Saved workplace coordinates can be included. You can disconnect in Ora or remove access in your Google Account. Erase all data also requests deletion from the linked account. If Drive cleanup is pending, reopen Ora and reconnect the same Google Account if prompted. Use Ora's erase action to apply the reset on other connected devices. Removing Ora's hidden app data directly in Drive removes the copy stored there at that time. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Location and notifications

Ora uses a precise location when you ask it to save a workplace. The saved coordinates stay with that job and can be included in backups and optional Drive sync. If you turn on workplace reminders and grant background location access, Android location services check when you arrive or leave even when Ora is closed. Ora uses these checks to remind you to clock in or out. You choose when to record work. You can turn reminders off, revoke location access in Android Settings, or remove the workplace from the job. Ora Pro offer notifications have a separate switch and are optional.

Lifetime purchases

Google Play handles payment. Ora uses Google Play and RevenueCat to check purchase status, grant or restore access, prevent purchase fraud and understand purchase activity. Ora can check access when you open the app. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app identifier, purchase tokens, purchase history and technical request information. Ora does not receive payment-card details or send your work records to RevenueCat. Play and App Store purchases are separate. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Google Play also checks the source of the app installation to help ensure it came through Play. That check is separate from Ora Pro and does not use your work records.

Exchange rates, device authentication and support

Ora may download public reference rates from the European Central Bank. The connection exposes standard network information such as your IP address. It does not send your jobs, amounts, or selected currencies. Ora keeps a local rates copy for offline conversions. Android handles fingerprint, face or device credential authentication when you choose to use it to reveal amounts.

If you email support, Selora receives your email and the information you choose to include. We keep support emails until we delete them or you ask us to delete them. To request access to or deletion of support or purchase-service information, email support@selora.dev and share only what is necessary to locate it. Clearing Ora's storage leaves Google Play purchase records and copies you saved elsewhere.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date. Questions about privacy? Email support@selora.dev.
