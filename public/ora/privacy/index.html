Privacy Policy

Ora · by Selora · last updated 14 July 2026

The short version: Ora does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

What Ora stores, and where

Everything you enter (jobs, pay rates, shifts, breaks, tips, notes, and settings) is stored locally on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync (optional), that same data is also stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, and syncs only between your devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

We (Selora) cannot see, access, or retrieve any of this data. It is yours.

Ora has no servers and no user accounts. Your data is never sent to us.

Network access

Ora makes exactly one kind of outbound request: it fetches public foreign-exchange rates from the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu) to show your earnings in a home currency. This request contains no personal data. It only downloads a public rates file, and you can avoid it entirely by not using the home-currency feature.

Ora contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no crash-reporting services.

Purchases

Ora Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Ora never sees your name, card, or Apple ID. Purchase status is verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payments.

Data collection summary

Ora's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected." The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Children

Ora is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
