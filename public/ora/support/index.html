Ora · by Selora

Ora is a calm shift & earnings tracker for iPhone and iPad: log your shifts, get the overnight, overtime, night and weekend math right, and always know what you've earned. One-time purchase, no subscription, no ads, no tracking.

Need help? Email support@selora.dev.

Please include your iOS version and device model if you're reporting a problem.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device (and your own iCloud, if you enable sync). Ora has no servers and no accounts, so we can't see your data. See the Privacy Policy.

What does Ora Pro unlock?

A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited jobs, the overtime / night / weekend / holiday pay rules, home-currency conversion, charts & breakdowns, PDF and CSV export, and widgets and Live Activity. Clocking in and out, breaks, tips, your earnings on any date, and iCloud sync are free. Ora Pro is a single payment, never a subscription, and is shared with your family via Family Sharing.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchase, making sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data → Export backup (JSON) for a full backup (free), or Export (Pro) for CSV and a PDF timesheet.

How is my pay calculated?

Ora computes base, overtime, night, weekend, and holiday pay from the rules you set per job. All amounts are estimates to help you track your earnings. They are not payslips and are not tax or legal advice.
