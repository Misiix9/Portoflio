Privacy Policy

Rentava · by Selora · last updated 30 August 2026

The short version: your rental records stay on your device unless you choose to turn on backup. Rentava has no access to your tenants' details, sells nothing to anyone, and shows no advertising.

What Rentava stores, and where

Everything you enter, properties, units, tenancies, rent charges, payments, deposits, expenses, maintenance jobs, contacts, documents and reminders, is written to a database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. The app works fully offline and asks for no account to use it.

By default, we cannot see, access or retrieve any of this. It never leaves your device.

There is no bank connection, and your tenants are never invited to anything or contacted by us.

Optional backup and sync

If you choose to connect an account, Rentava can back up your records so they survive a lost phone and appear on your other devices. This is off until you turn it on, from More ▸ Account.

When it is on, your records are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), operated by Google on our behalf as our data processor, and are readable only by your own signed-in account. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication using Sign in with Apple or an email address. We use the account identifier only to keep your data separate from every other user's.

You can turn backup off at any time, and you can delete your account and its stored copy from within the app. Deleting removes the cloud copy; the records already on your device stay there until you delete the app.

Information about your tenants

Names, email addresses, phone numbers and tenancy details you record about other people are your business records. Rentava stores them for you and does nothing else with them: they are never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never used to train anything. If you are in the UK or the EU, you are the data controller for those records and Selora is your processor for whatever you choose to back up.

Analytics and crash reports

Rentava sends a small amount of anonymous usage data through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, so we can tell which screens people use and see crashes we would otherwise never hear about. These events describe how the app was used, never what you recorded: for example that a paywall was shown, that a purchase completed, or that onboarding finished. No property address, tenant name, payment amount or document ever appears in them.

Crash reports contain the technical state of the app at the moment it stopped, plus a device model and operating system version. Rentava does not use the App Tracking Transparency identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Rentava Plus and Rentava Portfolio are subscriptions sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple Account details. Subscription status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase identifier and the receipt Apple issues, and returns whether the subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Photos, camera and notifications

Rentava asks for the photo library or camera only at the moment you attach a receipt or a property photo, and it reads only the images you pick. Attachments are stored with your records and follow the same rules as everything else. Notifications are used solely for reminders you created yourself, and Rentava asks for permission only when you set your first one.

Your rights

Because your records are on your device, you already hold them. You can export your data from the app at any time, and you can erase everything by deleting the app or, if backup is on, by deleting your account from More ▸ Account. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to access, correct, export and erase any personal data we hold, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to support@selora.dev and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Rentava is a tool for managing rental property and is not directed at children.
