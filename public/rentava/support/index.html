Common questions

A tenant paid part of the rent. Can I record that?

Yes. Record the amount that actually arrived. Rentava keeps the charge open, shows the remaining balance, and adds the rest when the next payment comes in. You never have to round a payment up or invent a figure to make a month balance.

Do I need an account?

No. Rentava works fully offline and asks for nothing to get started. An account is only needed if you want encrypted backup and syncing between your iPhone and iPad.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, in the app's own storage. If you turn on backup, a copy is also kept in your private area of our cloud so you can restore it. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open the More tab, tap Cloud account, then tap Delete account at the bottom. That erases your backed-up records and your sign-in permanently. Records already on your device are kept, so the app keeps working offline; deleting the app removes those too.

What do the plans include?

Free — one property with one rentable unit, and up to ten stored documents.

Plus — up to ten rentable units, unlimited documents, encrypted backup and sync across your devices, and the full set of reports.

Portfolio — up to thirty rentable units, everything in Plus, portfolio-wide reporting, and up to three collaborators.

Plans are monthly or yearly, and the yearly price is lower per month. You can change or cancel at any time in your Apple subscription settings.

I paid but the app still says Free

Open the More tab, tap Manage subscription, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us.

How do I cancel?

Cancelling is handled by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Rentava. Your paid features stay available until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Apple processes all refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell us as well if something went wrong, so we can fix it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, on every plan including Free. Open More → Export data to export everything as JSON and CSV, then share it anywhere iOS can send a file, including straight to an accountant.

Something is wrong, or a translation reads badly

Please tell us. Rentava is available in nine languages, and wording reports from people who actually speak the language are the most useful messages we get.

Still stuck?

Email support@selora.dev. Include what you expected to happen and what happened instead, and we will take it from there.
